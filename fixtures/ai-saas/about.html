We help teams do less busywork

Nimbus started in 2021 when three engineers got tired of copying data between spreadsheets.

We are a distributed team of 40 people building software for teams that would rather spend their time on the work itself. Our product is shaped by the people who use it every day.

Our first customers were small agencies and operations teams. They taught us that automation only matters if people trust it, so we built Nimbus to show its work.
